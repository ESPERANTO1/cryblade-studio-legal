## English

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Soft Blocks** (the “Game”), developed and published by **CRYBLADE Studio** (“we”, “us”, or “our”), handles information when you use the Game.

The current version of the Game is available for Android. We designed it to work without creating an account and without sending gameplay progress to our own servers.

### 2. Information handled by the Game

The Game stores only the information required for its features directly on your device. This may include:

- game progress, unlocked levels, and best scores;
- saved or unfinished game state;
- selected language;
- sound, music, and other game settings;
- locally tracked gameplay values, such as the number and cooldown of continue attempts.

This information is not intended to identify you and is not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by CRYBLADE Studio.

### 3. Information we do not currently collect

The current version of the Game does not provide user accounts and does not actively use advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, cloud-save, or in-app-purchase services. We do not currently collect or share your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, advertising identifier, payment information, or gameplay data through the Game.

If these features are added in a future version, we will update this Privacy Policy and the relevant disclosures before or when the changes take effect.

### 4. Google Play and device services

When you download or use the Game through Google Play or an Android device, Google or your device manufacturer may process certain information under their own terms and privacy policies. This may include technical, diagnostic, transaction, or account information handled by those services. CRYBLADE Studio does not control their independent processing.

For more information, please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Because the current version does not send personal information or gameplay progress to CRYBLADE Studio, we do not share such information with third parties through the Game.

### 6. Data retention and deletion

Game data described in Section 2 remains locally on your device until it is overwritten, you clear the Game’s storage, or you uninstall the Game. You can delete this local data through Android application settings or by uninstalling the Game.

We do not currently maintain an online player account or server-side profile that requires a separate deletion request.

### 7. Security

We limit the current Game to local storage and avoid collecting personal information through our own systems. However, no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and Google account.

### 8. Children’s privacy

Soft Blocks is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game. If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where applicable.

### 9. International users

CRYBLADE Studio is based in Ukraine. Because the current version does not transmit gameplay progress or personal information to our own servers, we do not currently transfer that information internationally through the Game. Third-party platform providers may process information in other countries under their own policies.

### 10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, legal requirements, or our data practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice in the Game or through its store listing.

### 11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact:
